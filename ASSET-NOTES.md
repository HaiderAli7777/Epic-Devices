## New website artwork

The built-in image-generation tool generated the following conceptual, unbranded marketing assets. They illustrate categories, not verified products for sale. The website uses optimized WebP copies.

- `assets/epic-hero.webp`: a cinematic studio composition of graphite headphones, a silver laptop and compact charger against a dark-blue sculptural background.
- `assets/epic-collections.webp`: a six-cell photograph atlas, in the order laptop, headphones, keyboard, mouse, charger and USB-C hub. The website positions the original cells in CSS without cutting them into separate images.

Hero prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create one premium electronics retail hero photograph for EPIC DEVICES, to be integrated into an actual website, not a screenshot or web mockup. Wide cinematic 1536x1024 landscape. Exceptionally realistic studio still life: matte graphite over-ear headphones floating diagonally above a low black polished circular plinth, a slim silver laptop partly open behind them on the right, and a small brushed titanium charging cube in the foreground. Unbranded generic conceptual devices. Large matte dark-blue circular disk backdrop, near-black navy studio. Subjects on the right two-thirds; dark negative space on the left. Cobalt rim light, icy white keylight, detailed aluminum and leather, subtle fog and realistic reflections. No text, logos, watermarks, UI or people.

Collection prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Create a 1536x1024 premium electronics category-art sprite atlas. Exactly six equal square cells in a three-column, two-row grid; no lines, labels or text. Identical pale cool gray backgrounds, natural contact shadows and soft studio light. Unbranded generic devices, fully contained within their cells, with generous padding. Top left: partly open silver laptop. Top center: graphite over-ear headphones. Top right: compact black mechanical keyboard viewed diagonally from above. Bottom left: graphite ergonomic mouse. Bottom center: black two-port USB-C charging brick. Bottom right: brushed silver USB-C hub with short cable. Photorealistic gray, black and silver materials. No brand names, logos or neon illustration style.

## Local fonts

Inter and Sora are distributed with their SIL Open Font License files in `assets/fonts/`. Variable font subsets cover Latin characters and weights 400–800. The supplied alternative theme definitions remain available and may load their fonts from Google Fonts when selected.
